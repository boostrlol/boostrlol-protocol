# BOOSTR Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

Attention is the scarcest thing a creator produces, and most of the value it generates never finds its way back to them. BOOSTR exists to close that gap. Our mission is to make creator support a by-product of ordinary market activity: when a token trades, the person it stands for gets paid, in the open, with a receipt anyone can check.

## How it works

1. A token that already trades on Solana is chosen.
2. A public handle is attached to it.
3. Trading fees accrue while the market runs.
4. Fees are forwarded to the creator, and the transfer is verifiable onchain.

## Roadmap

**Phase 1: Foundation.** Launch the public site, the live market board and the contract address slot. Publish the register of every external fact.

**Phase 2: Pairing.** Open handle pairing for existing Solana tokens and publish the first verifiable routes.

**Phase 3: Visibility.** Add a creator directory, route receipts and an analytics view of fees sent.

**Phase 4: Expansion.** Open the route format to partners and integrations, with creator-controlled settings.
